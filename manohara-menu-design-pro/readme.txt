Pro add-on for Manohara Menu Design: mega menus, a grid builder, icons, badges, WooCommerce parts and header features.

== Description ==

Manohara Menu Design Pro needs the free Manohara Menu Design plugin. Enter your license key under Appearance › Manohara Pro license.

= Mega menus =

Open Appearance › Menus, expand a top-level item and tick "Mega menu".

* 19 ready designs: icon cards, a platform grid with intro, side tabs with badges, features with a showcase, vertical tabs, classic columns, dark icon tiles, store columns with a banner, underlined headings, image cards, resources with an insight card, columns with a best seller, category tabs with products, a products panel with what's new, side tabs with a carousel, product lists with a promo, fashion lists with image tiles, a dark grid with a gradient promo, and wide columns with a footer row.
* Built from your menu: second-level items become columns, cards or tabs, third-level items their links. Fourth-level items under a tab become grouped columns.
* Intro text with two buttons, a promo card (image, title, text, price, button) and a footer strip, for the designs that show them.
* Width: the content width, the full screen width or a fixed width. The panel always stays on the screen.
* Grid builder: rows of 12 column units, with headings, links, a menu, text, images, buttons, promo banners, videos (YouTube, Vimeo or a file), social links, latest posts, shortcodes, block editor synced patterns and Elementor templates, and with WooCommerce: products, product categories and the cart. Drag rows and blocks to move them.
* Or use one synced pattern (block editor) or Elementor template as the whole panel.
* Phones and tablets: in the menu button's panel, mega menus open as accordions with everything in one column; tabs are shown one after another.

= Menu items =

* Icons: 57 built-in line icons, Dashicons, Font Awesome classes (when your theme or Elementor loads Font Awesome) or an image from the media library.
* An image for cards, carousels and product tiles.
* A badge (New, Popular, -20%) in any colour.
* The item's description under its label.
* Hide an item on desktop, tablet or mobile.

= Header and menu button (per device, on the design screen) =

* Sticky header: stays at the top, stays and gets smaller, or hides when scrolling down and comes back when scrolling up.
* Eight menu button styles with their own animations, and text next to the button.
* A different logo on this device (for example a small mobile logo).
* Side panel width.
* Swipe the panel to close it.
* Tap to open: on touch screens, the first tap on a parent item opens its submenu and the second follows the link.
* Hide page elements on a device (CSS selectors), for example a second theme menu or a desktop-only bar.
* The breakpoints for desktop, tablet and mobile are set in the free plugin's settings and are used by all Pro features.

= WooCommerce =

* Cart button with the number of items, updated when a product is added, and a My account button.
* The count on menu items that link to the cart page.
* Product and product category grids in mega menus.

= Multilingual sites =

Works with Vacanam Language Switcher, Polylang, WPML and TranslatePress, and with right-to-left languages.

* Each language's menu keeps its own icons, badges and mega menus. When Vacanam copies a menu for a new language, the item settings are copied too, so you only translate the texts.
* With WPML, translated items without settings of their own use the original item's settings.
* Synced patterns and Elementor templates in panels are shown in the visitor's language when they are translated.
* The text next to the menu button and the device logo are registered for string translation.
