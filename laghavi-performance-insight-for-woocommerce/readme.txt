Cookieless WooCommerce analytics with customer journeys: where each buyer came from, every product they viewed and for how long. No cookies.

== Description ==

**See what your WooCommerce store's visitors do — and what turns them into orders — without Google Analytics, cookies or a consent banner.**

Performance Insight lives right inside your **WooCommerce** menu, next to Orders and Products. Install it, activate it, and your store stats start counting with the next visit. No account, no tracking code, no setup.

= Answers in one screen =

* How many people visited today, and how many of them bought?
* **Where did this customer come from, which products did they look at, and for how long — before they bought, left their cart or signed up?**
* Which products get views but no sales?
* Where do your buyers come from — Google, Instagram, ads, email or **ChatGPT**?
* Which coupons bring sales, and which ones nobody uses?
* What is out of stock or running low — and is a sold-out product still getting visitors?
* Which pages and products have SEO problems, and what exactly to fix?

= Customer journeys — new in 1.4 =

For every visit that ends in an **order**, an **abandoned cart** or a **new customer account**, Performance Insight shows the whole path:

* **Where the customer came from** — Google or Bing (organic), paid ads (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google Ads, with the UTM campaign), social media, email, **ChatGPT and other AI assistants**, or direct.
* **Every page and product they looked at, in order, with the time spent on each** — e.g. Home 12s → Linen Kaftan 1m 35s → Silk Scarf 48s → order.
* **The outcome** — ordered (with the order number and value), abandoned cart (with the products left behind) or account created.
* **Per source**: orders, abandoned carts, cart-to-order rate, pages and time before buying.
* **Products looked at before buying vs before leaving** — find the products people study for minutes and then abandon.
* **On every order screen**: a "Customer journey" box showing where that customer came from and what they viewed before ordering.

Journeys are anonymous: no name, email or IP address is stored, and an order is linked only by its order number.

= Subscriptions, licenses, languages and logins — new in 1.5 =

When these plugins are active, Performance Insight adds their numbers to its own screens, read-only:

* **Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses** (and Pro): active, new and cancelled subscriptions, MRR, ARR, churn, net revenue retention, successful, failed and recovered renewals, trials and trial-to-paid conversion, revenue per currency, refunds and credit notes, licenses and activations, and "Ask for price" enquiries.
* **Rinnovo extensions**, each in its own tab when installed: subscription boxes (shipped, skips, swaps, prepaid and gift plans, expected quantities), membership content (members, courses, download credits), media archives (plays, watch time, quotas, devices), service contracts (contract MRR, quota used vs included, requests), session membership (bookings, attendance, no-shows, class utilisation, credits, waitlists) and bundles (components, held renewals, plan switches).
* **Vacanam Language Switcher**: views, orders, revenue and conversion per language, language changes and country redirects.
* **Sarala Login Page**: sign-ins and sign-ups per provider, failed logins and lockouts.

To-do cards point out what needs attention: failed renewals to follow up, trials ending this week, contracts close to their quota, classes under 40% full, box items that may run out before the next shipping day, and languages with traffic but low conversion. Visible to shop managers and administrators only.

= WooCommerce analytics, free =


* **Dashboard** — visitors, page views, visits, bounce rate, time per visit, orders, sales and **conversion rate**, compared with the period before.
* **Real-time visitors** — who is on your store in the last 30 minutes and what they are looking at.
* **Store sales funnel** — product views → added to cart → checkout → orders, so you see where shoppers drop off.
* **Best-selling products** — the top 100 products with visitors, orders, units, sales and conversion (physical, digital and subscription products).
* **Traffic sources** — search engines, social networks, **AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude…)**, other websites and UTM campaigns, with orders and sales per channel from WooCommerce order attribution.
* **Stock tracking** — live stock for every product and variation, **low-stock and out-of-stock alerts**, days of stock left at the current sales pace, sold-out products that still get visitors, and a direct **Edit** link for each one. Optional: **hide sold-out products from the shop automatically** and show them again when they are back in stock, plus a daily stock alert email.
* **Inside WooCommerce's own screens** — Visitors and Conversion columns in the Products list, a 30-day box (visitors, add to cart, orders, sales, sources) on every product's edit page, and the customer journey on every order.
* **Weekly store report email** — every Monday: visitors, orders, sales and conversion vs the week before, the best source, top products, abandoned carts and low stock.
* **Today in the admin toolbar** and a **WordPress Dashboard widget** — visitors and orders today, who is on the site now, and the latest customer journeys.
* **AI crawlers & search bots** — which AI companies (OpenAI GPTBot and ChatGPT, Anthropic ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Meta AI, Amazon and more) and search engines read your store, and which pages they read most.
* **Coupons report** — sales and discount per code, new vs returning customers, average order with vs without a coupon, and coupons nobody used.
* **Visitors** — countries, devices, browsers, operating systems, screen sizes and languages.
* **Pages & posts** — most visited pages, entry and exit pages, site search terms and **404 pages not found**.
* **SEO score for every page, post and product** — weakest first, with a checklist (title, meta description, text length, image alt text, headings, links, photos, reviews, SKU, stock). Works with Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO, SEOPress and Elementor.
* **A detail screen for every page and product** — visitors per day, sources, mobile vs desktop, add-to-carts, sales and its SEO problems.
* **Any period** — today, yesterday, 7 or 30 days, this or last month, this year or custom dates.
* **CSV export** of every report.

= Privacy friendly, GDPR friendly =

* **No cookies** and **no IP addresses** stored. A visitor is counted once per day with a one-way code that changes every day.
* Country comes from the browser's time zone — no IP lookup, no outside service.
* Raw page views are kept for 4 days only; after that only daily totals remain.
* Your own staff (administrators, shop managers — you choose) are not counted, bots are filtered and "Do Not Track" is respected.
* **Everything stays in your own WordPress database.** The only thing that can ever leave your site is the optional feedback form when you deactivate the plugin — and only if you press “Submit & Deactivate”.

= Light and fast =

Your pages are not changed. A tiny script (about 1 KB) sends one small request after the page has loaded. No jQuery, no external scripts, no slowdown.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for "Performance Insight WooCommerce analytics" and click **Install Now**, then **Activate**. (WooCommerce must be active.)
2. Open **WooCommerce → Performance Insight**.
3. That's it — counting starts with the next visit.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Where do I find the reports? =

In **WooCommerce → Performance Insight**. All screens (Visitors, Sources, Pages & posts, Journeys, Products, Coupons, Stock, SEO scores, Settings) are in the tab bar at the top of the page.

= Is this a Google Analytics alternative for WooCommerce? =

For most store owners, yes: visitors, sources, the sales funnel, products, coupons and conversion rate in one simple screen inside WordPress — without cookies, consent banners or a Google account. If you also use Google Analytics, both can run side by side.

= Do I need WooCommerce? =

Yes. Orders, sales, products, the funnel and coupons come from WooCommerce, so WordPress asks for WooCommerce to be active first.

= Does it work with Rinnovo, Vacanam and Sarala? =

Yes. When one of them is active, its tab appears automatically (Rinnovo, Languages or Logins). Each Rinnovo extension gets its own sub-tab only when it is installed. The data is read-only and cached for 15 minutes; nothing in those plugins is changed. All of it is free — nothing is limited by the Pro add-on.

= Does Performance Insight recommend other plugins? =

Yes, our own free plugins, on Performance Insight's own screens only (never on other admin pages, no pop-ups). The Dashboard and Settings show a small "More free plugins from Laghavi Digital" card with Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses and Sarala Login Page. Some screens also suggest one when your data points to it — for example when customers re-buy the same product (subscriptions) or carts are abandoned (one-click sign-in at checkout). Plugins you already have are never suggested, and every card has a "Not now" link that hides it.

= What is a customer journey? =

The path one visitor took on your store in one visit: where they came from (search, ads, social, email, AI assistant or direct), each page and product they opened with the time spent on it, and how it ended — an order, an abandoned cart or a new account. Find them in **WooCommerce → Performance Insight → Journeys**, and on each order's edit screen.

= Are customer journeys GDPR friendly? =

They are stored without cookies, names, email addresses or IP addresses. A journey is linked to an order only by the order number, which is already in your store. Journeys are kept for 90 days by default (Settings) and removed with the plugin.

= Can I see which AI bots read my site? =

Yes. The Sources screen lists AI crawlers (GPTBot, ChatGPT, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Meta AI, Amazonbot and others) and search engine crawlers, and the pages AI crawlers read most. Bots are never counted as visitors. Pages served from a full-page cache may not be counted.

= Can I see traffic from ChatGPT and other AI assistants? =

Yes. When someone clicks a link to your store inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude or similar, the visit is counted under **AI assistants** with the assistant's name — and you see the orders it brought.

= Does it slow my site down? =

No. Pages are not changed. A tiny script (about 1 KB) sends one small request after the page has loaded, and one more with the time spent when the visitor leaves.

= Do I need a cookie banner for this plugin? =

The plugin sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses. Check your local law and your own privacy policy, but this plugin is built to need no consent for counting.

= Is my data sent anywhere? =

No. Everything stays in your WordPress database. The optional deactivation feedback form sends only what you choose to type (see External services). Only the separate Performance Insight Pro add-on (part of a paid Laghavi Ads Hub plan) sends data, and only after you connect it.

= Will numbers match Google Analytics? =

Not exactly. Every analytics tool counts a little differently (ad blockers, bots, consent banners, staff visits). Use the numbers to compare your own pages, products and periods with each other.

= How long is data kept? =

Daily totals are kept as long as you choose in **Settings** (default 400 days). Raw page views are kept for 4 days only.

= Does it work with HPOS, Elementor and SEO plugins? =

Yes — WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage, Elementor pages and Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO and SEOPress are supported.

= What happens when I delete the plugin? =

By default all its tables and settings are removed. You can switch that off in **Settings**.

= Why are some numbers empty right after I install it? =

Visits, page views and time on page can only be counted from the moment the plugin is switched on. Everything WooCommerce already knows is brought in straight away: on activation the plugin reads your past orders and unfinished checkouts (with WooCommerce's order attribution — source, landing page, pages seen, device) and shows them under Journeys and Sources, marked "from WooCommerce's order record". Sales, products, coupons, stock and SEO already use your full store history.

== External services ==

This plugin contacts one outside service, and only when you choose to:

* **Deactivation feedback (optional).** When you click "Deactivate" on the Plugins screen, a short form asks why. If you press "Submit & Deactivate", the plugin sends the reason you picked, the text you typed, your email and site address only if you entered or ticked them, and the plugin, WordPress, WooCommerce and PHP versions to Laghavi Digital at https://sh.laghavi.site/feedback, so we can fix what went wrong and reply if you asked. "Skip & Deactivate" sends nothing. [Privacy policy](https://laghavi.site/privacy-policy/)

The review request link opens WordPress.org in your browser; the plugin itself sends nothing.
