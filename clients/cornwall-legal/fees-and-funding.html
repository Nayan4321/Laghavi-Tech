Funded services

Funded services, sometimes called 'Legal Aid'

We work with trusted firms of solicitors and law centres to offer referrals and early legal advice on issues involving housing, debt, welfare benefits and family law. The funding rules are complex, so we will assess your income and try our best to ensure you qualify. Where the funding application is complex, we would ask that you donate £25 to us, to cover our time.
