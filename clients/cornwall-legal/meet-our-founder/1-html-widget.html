About the episode

We're excited to share a podcast episode from Real Start Up Stories featuring an in-depth conversation with the founder of Cornwall Legal Centre.

In this episode, you'll hear about:

  • what inspired the creation of our community interest company
  • the kinds of legal challenges people in Cornwall commonly face
  • why getting the right support early can make all the difference
  • the practical, everyday situations where people often don't realise they need legal help, until it's too late
Whether you're dealing with a housing issue, an employment dispute, or simply unsure where to turn, this conversation offers helpful insight into how we work and how we can support you.
