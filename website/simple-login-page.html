Questions

Do I need WooCommerce?

No. Without WooCommerce the buttons appear on the WordPress login and registration pages, and anywhere you add the shortcode. With WooCommerce, new sign-ups become customers and guests can sign up at checkout.

Someone already has an account. What happens when they use Google?

If Google (or another provider) confirms the same email address, they are logged into their existing account and Google is connected to it. If the email isn't verified, they log in with their password first, so nobody can take over an account.

Do I need developer accounts with Google, Facebook and the others?

Yes, a free app with each provider you want to use. You only enable the ones you need, and the settings page shows the exact address to paste into each provider.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Nothing is sent to Laghavi Digital. A visitor's browser only talks to the login provider they choose. To show the location of failed logins, the IP address is looked up with IPinfo.

Does it slow my site down?

No. Its small stylesheet only loads on pages that show the login buttons, and nothing runs on the rest of your site.

What do I get with Pro?

Five login page templates with your branding, matching forgot and reset password pages, a protected super admin, a full user activity log and per-role control over admin menus, editor blocks, custom fields, widgets and front-end content. You get the download and your license key in My Account straight after paying.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time from My Account. Your subscription won't renew, and your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.
