      <details><summary>Can I change only my mobile menu?</summary><p>Yes. Leave Desktop and Tablet on "Theme design" and choose a template for Mobile. Your theme's menu stays as it is on the larger screens.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Can each device show a different menu?</summary><p>Yes. Each device has its own "Menu on this device" setting. Make the menus in Appearance › Menus.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it work with my theme?</summary><p>Yes. It works with classic themes through their menu locations, with block themes through the block, and with any theme through the shortcode or the Elementor widget. When a device shows Manohara's own header bar, the theme's header is hidden on that device; the common themes are covered, and you can add your theme's header selector in the settings.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it slow my site down?</summary><p>No. The script is about 7 KB without jQuery, only the styles of the templates you use are loaded, and nothing is loaded from other websites.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it work with page caching?</summary><p>Yes. Devices are told apart by screen width in CSS, so cached pages are the same for everyone.</p></details>
