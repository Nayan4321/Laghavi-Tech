Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Your website in every language, without translating it yourself

The free Vacanam Language Switcher copies every page into each language with its design and connects the translations. Vacanam Pro does the translating for you, makes your shop truly multilingual and gives you the tools growing sites need. Everything in the free plugin stays free.

Translate with AI

  • Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or DeepL with your own key: you pay the service directly, with no word limits from us.
  • Pages, posts, products, categories, menus, site texts, Elementor designs and Yoast, Rank Math and SEOPress titles and descriptions.
  • One page from the editor, or the whole site in one click; stop and continue any time.
  • Your tone, a short description of your business, Modern Standard or Gulf Arabic, and a glossary for brand names and your own words.
  • Translation memory: the same sentence is never paid for twice.
  • Text inside banners and images redrawn in the page's language; you approve each image before it is used.

Addresses that rank

  • Translated slugs (/ar/منتج/حذاء/) or the same slug in every language.
  • Product, category and tag bases translated.
  • A sub-domain (ar.yoursite.com) or a separate domain per language.
  • Site-wide SEO title templates of Yoast SEO, Rank Math and SEOPress translated.

Every text of your theme and plugins

  • String translation finds every text your theme and plugins can show by reading their code, including WooCommerce.
  • Translate on one screen, with AI, or import and export PO and XLIFF files for translators.
  • Texts saved in settings (footer texts, email texts) too.
  • Translation status everywhere: complete, partly translated, original changed or missing.

WooCommerce in every language

  • Variable and grouped products copied with their variations, attributes, upsells and cross-sells.
  • One price and one stock for every translation: an order in Arabic takes from the same stock.
  • The cart follows the visitor's language, even across domains.
  • Customer emails and PDF invoices in the language the order was placed in; the order language in your orders list.
  • Shop, cart, checkout and account pages per language; "Copy into all languages" under each product.

A currency per language and country targeting

  • Arabic in AED, English in USD: a currency per language, switched off until you want it.
  • Works with the currency switcher of Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses Pro, or converts prices by itself with your rates and rounding.
  • First visit opens the language of the visitor's country; their own choice always wins, and admins and search engines are never redirected.
  • Content shown or hidden by country.

Forms, login pages and switchers

  • Contact Form 7 and WPForms forms per language, with their messages and emails.
  • Login, lost-password and sign-up pages in the visitor's language, including Sarala Login Page; account emails in the language people signed up in.
  • A first-visit language suggestion or full-screen chooser, a bottom sheet on phones, and a language and currency switcher.
  • Content loaded by scripts (filters, popups, mini-carts) keeps the language.

Language analytics and developer tools

  • Views, language changes, country redirects, visitors who leave right after switching, and orders, revenue and conversion per language. No cookies, nothing personal.
  • REST API, JavaScript events, hooks and custom language providers. WPML and Polylang functions keep working.

What you need

  • The free Vacanam Language Switcher (WordPress 6.2 or later, PHP 7.4 or later).
  • For AI translation: an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or DeepL.
