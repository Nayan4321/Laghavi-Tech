1Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the rinnovo-membership-content-pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › Membership Pro license. Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

3First setup: your first membership

  1. Create the products: for example "Gold Monthly" and "Gold Yearly" as subscriptions (Product data › General › Subscription).
  2. WooCommerce › Membership › Tiers and perks: add a tier (for example Gold), set its Group, choose the products under "Products that give this tier". Save.
  3. Open a post or page, find the Members only box: Who can see this = Gold members and higher. Optionally "Unlock after (days of membership)". Update.
  4. WooCommerce › Membership › Teasers and paywall: choose the Teaser and the button text. Save.
  5. Log out and open the post: you see the teaser and the paywall. Buy the product as a test customer: the post opens.

4Groups and tiers

A group is an independent programme (Business training, Photography, VIP community). Inside a group, tiers inherit from lower tiers (Gold includes Silver). A member can belong to several groups at once; access is worked out from all their active memberships, course purchases and manual grants together.

6Drip timeline

In the Members only box of a page or post, open it after a number of days of membership (Day 1 welcome, Day 7 module 2, Day 30 premium resources) or on a date. The drip timeline in My Membership shows members what opens next.

7Protected files and video

Files are never reachable by a public URL: downloads are checked on every request and served through the server (X-Sendfile or X-Accel when available), with no-store headers and limits per member or file.

ProviderHow it is protected
Bunny StreamPrivate library with token playback
VimeoPrivacy set to "hide from Vimeo" plus domain restriction to your site
WistiaWistia privacy controls
YouTube unlistedNot protected: anyone with the link can watch. Use it only for previews.

8Selling courses

  1. WooCommerce › Membership › Course & product mapping.
  2. Pick a product, then one or more courses (LearnDash, LifterLMS, or a category of course pages).
  3. Choose the access length (lifetime or months) and when it starts. Save.

Several courses on one product make a bundle; one course can be unlocked by several products (a standalone purchase or a Gold membership). The Courses and tiers matrix shows each course's price and which tiers include it. A course bought on its own stays when a membership ends.

9Download plans and credits

  1. WooCommerce › Membership › Download plans: credits per period, reset, carry-over, maximum per day and at the same time.
  2. Choose per-category quotas (photos 30, PSD 10, videos 5) or one shared wallet.
  3. Add resources (Resources menu) with a file, preview and credit cost.
  4. Sell top-ups as products with Download credits. Purchased credits are kept apart and never vanish with the monthly reset.

Members see their balance (32 of 50), reset date and download history in My Membership.
