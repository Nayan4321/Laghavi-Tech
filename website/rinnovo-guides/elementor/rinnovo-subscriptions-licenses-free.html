1Before you start

  • WordPress 6.2 or later and PHP 7.4 or later.
  • WooCommerce installed and set up (store address, currency, at least one shipping zone if you ship).
  • A payment method: Stripe (built in), or one of the card plugins listed in "Renewals with your own payment plugin", or bank transfer / cash (renewals are then paid by invoice).
  • Pretty permalinks: Settings › Permalinks must not be "Plain".

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses is complete on its own. It needs no license key and has no limits. The Pro plugins only add extra features.

2Install and activate

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New and search for "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses".
  2. Click Install now, then Activate.
  3. Go to Settings › Permalinks and click Save Changes once (nothing to change, saving refreshes the addresses used by My Account and the license API).
  4. A notice "Set up Stripe" appears if you have not set up Stripe yet. You can skip it if you use another payment plugin.

4First setup in 10 minutes

  1. Set up Stripe (next section) or check that your own payment plugin saves cards.
  2. Open WooCommerce › Settings › Subscriptions. The defaults are fine for most shops; read the table in "Every setting" before going live.
  3. Create a subscription product (see "Create a subscription product").
  4. Turn on Stripe Test mode and buy your own product with the card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future date, any CVC.
  5. Check WooCommerce › Subscriptions: your test subscription is Active with a next payment date.
  6. Open My Account › Subscriptions as the customer: you see the plan, next payment, Update card and Cancel.
  7. Turn Test mode off, paste the live key, and you are ready.

5Stripe step by step

  1. In Stripe go to Developers › API keys. Copy the Secret key (starts with sk_live_). For testing, also copy the test secret key (sk_test_).
  2. In WordPress go to WooCommerce › Settings › Payments and open Stripe.
  3. Tick Enable Stripe. Change Title and Description if you like (customers see them at checkout).
  4. Paste the key in Live secret key. For testing tick Test mode ("Use test keys (no real money moves)") and paste the key in Test secret key.
  5. Click Save changes. Rinnovo creates the Stripe webhook for you, and the Webhook line turns green: Connected.
SettingWhat it does
EnableShows Stripe at checkout.
Title / DescriptionThe name and text customers see for this payment method.
Live secret keyFrom Stripe Dashboard › Developers › API keys. Starts with sk_live_ (or rk_live_ for a restricted key).
Test modeUse test keys, no real money moves. Test card: 4242 4242 4242 4242.
Test secret keyYour sk_test_ key, used only while Test mode is on.
WebhookShows whether Stripe can tell your shop about payments. Created automatically when you save the key.
Webhook signing secret (optional)Only if the automatic webhook setup failed and you added the webhook in Stripe yourself. Starts with whsec_.

With Stripe, customers pay on Stripe's secure page (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, 3D Secure). Subscriptions become Stripe Billing subscriptions: Stripe charges each renewal and retries failed cards. Refunds work from the WooCommerce order screen.

Webhook not Connected? Your site must be reachable from the internet over https (a local or password-protected staging site cannot receive webhooks). See "Troubleshooting".

6Renewals with your own payment plugin

Already use a payment plugin? Rinnovo saves the card or bank mandate at the first payment and charges it on each renewal date through that plugin. A failed charge is tried again after 1 and 3 days, and the customer gets a "Pay now" link. Turn this on with "Automatic renewals with other payment plugins" in WooCommerce › Settings › Subscriptions (on by default).

Payment pluginRegionWhat to turn on
WooCommerce Stripe GatewayWorldwide, including UAE and IndiaTurn on "Saved cards" in the Stripe settings.
WooPaymentsUS, UK, EU, Australia and moreTurn on saved cards in the WooPayments settings.
PayPal PaymentsWorldwideTurn on vaulting (saving PayPal and cards) in the PayPal Payments settings.
SquareUS, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, EUAllow customers to save cards in the Square settings.
Braintree, Authorize.NetWorldwideAllow saved cards in the plugin's settings.
MollieEuropeCards, SEPA Direct Debit, iDEAL, Bancontact and other methods Mollie allows for recurring payments.
Checkout.comWorldwide, including UAETurn on "Save cards" in the Checkout.com settings.
GoCardlessUK and EU (bank direct debit)Works with the bank mandate from the first payment.
PaystackNigeria, Ghana, South Africa, KenyaTurn on "Saved cards" in the Paystack settings.

Every other payment method (bank transfer, cash on delivery, Razorpay and others that don't save cards) still works: on each renewal date the customer gets an invoice email with a "Pay now" link. The table "This shop" in WooCommerce › Settings › Subscriptions shows how each of your enabled payment methods renews: Automatic (Stripe Billing), Automatic (saved card or mandate) or Invoice email with a "Pay now" link.

7Create a subscription product

  1. Products › Add New. Give it a name and a Regular price (the amount charged every period).
  2. In Product data › General tick Subscription.
  3. Fill in Bill every and Billing period, for example 1 + month, or 3 + month for quarterly.
  4. Optional: Sign-up fee, Free trial (days), Number of payments.
  5. Publish. The price shows as "$99.00 / month · 7-day free trial · $20.00 sign-up fee" and the button says "Subscribe".
FieldWhat it does
SubscriptionCharge the price again every billing period.
Bill everyHow many periods between payments: 1 = every period, 3 = every third.
Billing periodDay, week, month or year.
Sign-up feeCharged once with the first payment, on top of the price.
Free trial (days)Days before the first charge. A reminder goes out before the trial ends.
Number of paymentsHow many times to charge. 0 = until cancelled. 12 with monthly = a one-year plan.

One subscription per order. Customers can buy other products together with it. Guests are asked to create an account so they can manage their subscription later.

8Monthly and yearly plans on one product

  1. Create a Variable product and add an attribute, for example "Plan" with the values Monthly | Yearly (tick "Used for variations").
  2. Variations › Generate variations.
  3. Open each variation, set its price and tick Subscription inside the variation.
  4. Set Billing period per variation: month for Monthly, year for Yearly. Each variation can have its own trial, sign-up fee and number of payments.
  5. Save. Customers pick their plan on the product page.

Selling software? Each variation can also have its own number of activations (License: activations per key), for example Single site, 5 sites, Unlimited. See the license keys guide.

10Managing subscriptions

WooCommerce › Subscriptions lists every subscription with the monthly recurring revenue at the top. Open one to see its orders ("First order", "Renewal"), how it renews ("Charged automatically on each renewal date" or "Invoice emailed on each renewal date") and these buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
Cancel at end of periodStops renewals. The customer keeps access until the paid period ends.
Cancel nowEnds it right away.
ResumeRestarts a cancelled (not yet ended) or on-hold subscription.
Charge renewal nowCharges the next renewal to the saved card now (automatic renewals).
Send renewal invoice nowEmails the "Pay now" invoice now (invoice renewals).
Mark as activeFor a subscription you know is paid (for example paid by bank transfer outside the shop).

A cancellation in My Account or here also cancels the subscription in Stripe. With other payment plugins nothing is charged after a cancellation, because Rinnovo creates every renewal charge itself.

11How a renewal works, day by day

WhenWhat happens
3 days before (Reminder before renewal)Reminder email: "Your subscription renews on …" (or "Your trial ends on …").
Renewal dateA renewal order is created. Stripe or the saved card is charged; otherwise the invoice email with "Pay now" is sent.
Charge failedTried again after 1 and 3 days. The customer gets "Payment failed" with an "Update card" button.
7 days after (put on hold after)Still unpaid: the subscription goes on hold and the overdue notice is sent.
30 days after (cancel after)Still unpaid: the subscription is cancelled. 0 = never cancel automatically.

Renewals run every hour in the background (WP-Cron). On low-traffic sites set up a real cron job in your hosting panel so they run on time (see Troubleshooting).

13Selling software and digital files

Tick Sell a license in a product's License tab and every paid order gets a license key, shown on the thank-you page, in the order email and in My Account › Licenses. You can upload the file customers download, set how many sites or devices a key works on, and give WordPress plugins and themes automatic updates.

The guide "Rinnovo license keys: connect your software" explains every field, each kind of product, where to put the license screen in your product, code examples in eight languages, every error message and how to release updates.

14Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Webhook is not ConnectedThe site must be public and on https. Save the Stripe settings again. If it still fails, add the webhook in Stripe › Developers › Webhooks yourself (the address is shown on the Stripe settings screen) and paste its whsec_ secret in Webhook signing secret.
Renewals are late or not createdWP-Cron only runs when someone visits the site. In your hosting panel add a cron job every 5–15 minutes: wget -q -O - https://your-store.com/wp-cron.php?doing_wp_cron, and add define( 'DISABLE_WP_CRON', true ); to wp-config.php.
Card plugin renewals become invoicesTurn on saved cards in the payment plugin (see the table above) and check "This shop" says Automatic. Orders paid before saved cards were on renew by invoice until the customer pays once with a saved card.
My Account › Subscriptions or Licenses shows "Page not found"Settings › Permalinks › Save Changes.
Customers can't change quantities on checkoutYour checkout page uses the Checkout block. Use the classic [woocommerce_checkout] shortcode, or accept that quantities are changed on the product page.
The Subscribe button still says "Add to cart"Your theme or page builder prints its own button. Check the product has Subscription ticked; for builders use the WooCommerce "Add to cart" widget, which uses the shop's button text.
Stripe test payment failsTest mode must be on with a sk_test_ key, and you must use a test card (4242 4242 4242 4242).
A customer was charged after cancellingA Stripe subscription created outside Rinnovo is not cancelled by it. Cancel it in the Stripe Dashboard and refund from the WooCommerce order.

15Questions people ask

QuestionAnswer
Do I need another subscription plugin?No. Rinnovo handles subscription products, renewals, emails and the customer pages itself.
Does it need a license key?No. The license keys are for the software you sell in your own shop.
What happens when I deactivate or delete it?Subscription and license records stay, because they are financial history. Stripe subscriptions keep charging until you cancel them in Stripe.
Can I move subscribers from another plugin?Ask us through the contact page: we help with imports.
What do the Pro plugins add?Invoices and tax invoices, trials and first-payment offers, VAT / GST, currencies, "Ask for price", and plugins for subscription boxes, memberships and courses, streaming, service contracts, classes and bundles.
