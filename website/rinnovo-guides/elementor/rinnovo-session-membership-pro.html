1Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the rinnovo-session-membership-pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › Session Membership Pro license. Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

3First setup: your first class

  1. Products › Add New: "Monthly 12 classes", tick Subscription, then in General tick Session membership and set Sessions per billing period to 12. Publish.
  2. Classes › Add class: Weekly times (one per line, for example "Mon 18:00"), Length, Places, Credits per booking 1. Publish.
  3. Create a page "Book a class" with [session_schedule]. Publish.
  4. WooCommerce › Sessions › Settings: set Booking opens and the Cancel / move cut-off. Save.
  5. Buy the membership as a test customer and book a class from the page.

5Families

Choose One shared pool for the family, A separate allowance per person, or both. Each person has their own bookings, attendance and waiver; you choose whether the account holder books for others and whether each person signs their own waiver.

6Schedules

Each class has weekly times, extra dates, a time zone, and blackout dates (holidays). Every date becomes its own session you can cancel or give a substitute instructor (booked members get their credits back and are told). Changing the times after people booked cancels the dates that disappear, returns the credits and tells them.

8Recurring bookings, check-in and reports

Members book a series (every Monday and Wednesday 6 PM for 4 weeks); it stops when credits run out, the membership ends or they stop it. Each booking has a QR code; staff scan it or type the short code on the session screen. Reports show utilisation, attendance, no-shows and late cancels per class, and the instructor roster with days instructors marked as away.

9Booking tools

WooCommerce Bookings works on its own hooks. For Amelia or Bookly, set the Adapter secret in settings and point their webhook to /wp-json/rinnovose/v1/booking with the header X-Rinnovose-Secret. Bookings take a credit, cancellations give it back, and duplicate deliveries are ignored.

10Session Pro or Membership Pro?

Membership Content decides "is this person a Gold member"; Session Pro decides "can they book this class and do they have credits". A Gold member may have no class credits, and someone can buy a 10-class pack without a membership.
