1Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the rinnovo-bundles-variable-subscriptions-pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › Bundles Pro license. Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

3First setup: your first bundle

  1. Products › Add New: tick Subscription in General (monthly).
  2. Product data › Bundle: tick Bundle, choose Contents (fixed components, mix and match, or box builder).
  3. Add the components (or the items to choose from with Minimum / Maximum items), choose Price (flat or dynamic) and Bundle discount. Publish.
  4. WooCommerce › Bundles & Plans: set Check stock and turn on Hold renewals. Save.
  5. Buy it as a test customer; the bundle and its components show on the order.

6Out of stock at renewal

When a critical component is out of stock, the renewal is held and not charged. Open Held bundle renewal and choose: replace the item, skip it, partial ship, delay (it retries), or refund the difference, then release the renewal. Optional components out of stock are skipped and the price adjusted.

7What customers can do

In My Account: change the next box only (Next shipment only), copy the previous box, save a favourite box, skip or replace one component for the next shipment.

8Variable plans and switching

The plan table on the product sets each variation's price, period, sign-up fee and trial; shoppers see a Compare plans table (also the Plan table widget / [bundle_plans]).

Example: Monthly $20 → Yearly $200 halfway through the month: the unused $10 is credited, the customer pays $190 now and the next renewal is in a year. Card subscriptions pay the difference at once; others get a credit on the next renewal.

9One renewal statement

Under Settings you can send one statement for several independent subscriptions renewing the same day. Each subscription keeps its own record, payment and cancellation.

10Which plugin does what
