1Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the rinnovo-physical-subscription-boxes-pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › Boxes Pro license. Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

3First setup: your first box

  1. Products › Add New. Set the price, tick Subscription in General (Billing period Month).
  2. Product data › Subscription box: tick Box subscription and tick the frequencies you offer (every 2, 4, 6 weeks…).
  3. Choose Price after a swap and Item out of stock at renewal (see the reference below).
  4. Product data › Box builder: pick the kind of box (Fixed, Scheduled, Build your own, Preference-based or Hybrid) and add its products. Publish.
  5. WooCommerce › Settings › Rinnovo › Subscription boxes: set One billing day, Order cut-off and Shipping on renewals. Save.
  6. Buy the box once as a test, then open WooCommerce › Boxes to see it under "To pack and ship".

5Frequencies

Tick the frequencies customers may choose (every week, 2, 4 or 6 weeks, every month, 3 months…). They appear as chips on the product. The frequency chosen replaces the product's default billing interval for that subscription; customers can switch later in My Account.

8Prepaid and gift plans

Prepaid boxes: the customer pays once for 3, 6 or 12 boxes (with a Prepaid discount). Each box becomes its own order on its delivery date; WooCommerce › Boxes › Prepaid shows boxes sent and left, and the next date.

Gift plans: at checkout the buyer adds the recipient's name and email, a start date and a gift message. The recipient gets an email and their own page with the deliveries; the plan ends after the last prepaid box.

9Shipping

Shipping on renewals uses your shipping zones. When a customer changes their address, the next renewal's shipping is recalculated for the new zone, and card subscriptions are updated with the new amount. With One billing day for everyone every box is billed on the same Day of the month (or Weekday) so you pack in one batch.
