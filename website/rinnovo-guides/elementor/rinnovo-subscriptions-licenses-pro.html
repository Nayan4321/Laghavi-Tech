1Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the rinnovo-subscriptions-licenses-pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses Pro (the license screen). Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

3First setup

  1. WooCommerce › Settings › Rinnovo › Trials and first-payment offers: tick Offers, choose "Card for free trials" and "Trials per customer" (one per customer is safest). Save.
  2. If you sell to businesses: VAT / GST section: tick Business tax numbers, choose Reverse charge, tick Check EU numbers with VIES and Location evidence. Save.
  3. Edit a subscription product: set Free trial (days) to 14 in General, and optionally a First payment discount. Update.
  4. Selling software: Product data › License: tick Sell a license, choose What you sell, set Product ID and Activations per license, upload the file. Update.
  5. Sell in more currencies: Currencies section: add your Extra currencies with a rate (or tick Update rates daily). Save.

4Free trials that convert

  1. On the product: set Free trial (days) and, if you like, a First payment discount (percent or fixed).
  2. Settings › Trials and first-payment offers: choose whether a trial needs a card (Card for free trials), Trials per customer (one per customer, one per product) and what happens when the First payment after a trial fails (cancel, or give some Days to pay after a trial).
  3. Customers see the trial end date and the first renewal amount on the product page, at checkout, in emails and in My Account. A reminder email goes out before the trial ends.

One trial per customer is checked by account, email address and, when the payment method shares it, the card's fingerprint.

5VAT and GST

Turn on Business tax numbers to add a VAT / GST number field at checkout. EU numbers can be checked with VIES (choose what happens When VIES is down).

Reverse charge is a separate decision from a valid number: the rules look at the customer's country, whether they are a business, where you are and what you sell. Every order stores the decision and the evidence used (billing address, IP address and more) under Location evidence, and each customer has a tax-exemption history.

7Licenses that follow the plan

Set License: sites per key on each variation (1, 5, 0 = unlimited). When a customer upgrades, downgrades or renews, the key's sites and expiry are updated and the change is written to the license history on the subscription.

8"Ask for price" and quote enquiries

  1. On the product tick Ask for price and write the Text shown instead of the price.
  2. Pick the buttons to show: your Contact page or URL, and WhatsApp (number and message).
  3. Every click is saved as an Enquiry with the product, variation, quantity and customer. Move it through new › contacted › quoted › won or lost, and turn a won enquiry into an order with one click.

10Settings: Trials and first-payment offers

WooCommerce › Settings › Rinnovo.

SettingWhat it does
OffersTurns trial and first-payment offers on.
Card for free trialsAsk for a card when a trial starts (charged when it ends). Without a card, the customer gets a "Pay now" invoice when the trial ends. Each product can override this.
Offer under the priceShows "First payment: $5.00" and "No card needed" under the product price.
Trials per customerOne free trial per customer for any product, one per product, or no limit. Checked by account and email address, so a new account with the same email gets no second trial.
First payment after a trial failsTreat it like any renewal (retries, grace, on hold, cancelled), or cancel after the days below because the trial was never paid.
Days to pay after a trialWith "Cancel": days the customer has to pay before the subscription is cancelled.

11Settings: VAT / GST for digital goods

SettingWhat it does
Business tax numbersAdds a VAT / GST number field for business customers at checkout and applies the rules below.
Reverse chargeEU businesses in another EU country (valid number), any business with a valid number in another country, or Off (always charge tax, only print the number). Reverse-charged orders, renewals and invoices say "Reverse charge".
Check EU numbers with VIESChecks EU numbers with the European Commission. UK, GSTIN, ABN, TRN and others are checked for the right format.
When VIES is downAccept numbers in the right format (the order is flagged for you) or charge VAT.
Location evidenceKeeps the billing country and IP country on each order and flags orders where they differ (EU digital goods rules).
Invoice noteText printed on reverse-charge invoices.

13Settings: Currencies

SettingWhat it does
Extra currenciesOne row per currency: the currency, its rate against your store currency, and the payment methods allowed for it. Leave the last row empty to add nothing.
Floating switcherA small currency picker in the bottom corner of every page.
Switcher on checkoutShows "Pay in USD / AED / …" above the payment methods.
Pick currency by countryOn a first visit, picks the currency listed for the visitor's country (needs Cloudflare, or MaxMind in WooCommerce › Settings › Integration). Visitors can still switch.
