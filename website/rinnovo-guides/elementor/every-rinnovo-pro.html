1Before you start

  • WordPress 6.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later.
  • At least one payment method set up in WooCommerce › Settings › Payments (Stripe, a card plugin, or bank transfer).
  • Your license key: My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site.

2Install and activate

Each Rinnovo Pro needs WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Your download from My Account is a package with two zips: the Pro plugin and the Rinnovo Box Builder (shared by all Rinnovo Pros).

  1. In WordPress go to Plugins › Add New, search "Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses", click Install now, then Activate.
  2. On your computer, unzip the package you downloaded. You get two zips.
  3. Plugins › Add New › Upload Plugin: upload the Pro zip and activate it. Do the same with rinnovo-box-builder.zip.
  4. Go to WooCommerce › (your Pro) license, for example WooCommerce › Boxes Pro license. Paste your license key from My Account › Licenses on laghavi.site and click Activate. Status turns Active and "Updates until" shows the end of your license year.
  5. Updates now arrive in Dashboard › Updates like any other plugin.

Already using another Rinnovo Pro? Skip step 1, and upload the Box Builder zip only if it is not installed yet.

4First setup in 10 minutes

  1. WooCommerce › Settings › Rinnovo: check "Reminder before renewal (days)" (3 is a good start) and the two "Unpaid invoice" days, then Save.
  2. WooCommerce › Invoices: fill Legal company name, Legal address and Tax registration number, pick a Template, tick "Create an invoice for every paid order" and "Attach the invoice PDF…", then Save. Click Preview to see a sample.
  3. Products › Add New: give it a price, tick Subscription in Product data › General, set Bill every 1 and Billing period Month, then Publish.
  4. Open the product on your site and buy it with a test payment. Check WooCommerce › Subscriptions and the invoice in My Account › Invoices.
  5. Now open your Pro's own guide for its settings.

5Invoices, set up once for all Pros

Go to WooCommerce › Invoices. Whichever Pros you use, there is one invoice engine: one invoice per order, one number sequence, one settings screen.

  1. Company: legal name, address, logo, tax number (TRN, GSTIN or VAT number) and the label printed before it.
  2. Numbering: prefix, digits and next number. Add {fy} to restart numbering each financial year, for example INV-{fy}-0001.
  3. Template: pick one of three designs and a colour, then preview.
  4. Customer details: whether business customers can, must or cannot add a company name and tax number at checkout.
  5. Emails: attach the PDF to order emails, or add a download link.
Where you sellWhat the invoice shows
India (GST)GSTIN, SAC/HSN codes per product, CGST + SGST inside your state, IGST between states, export without GST
UAE (VAT)TRN, "Tax Invoice" title, VAT per line and total in AED
EU / UK (VAT)VAT number, reverse charge note when it applies, VAT per rate
Anywhere elseA plain invoice with your company details

Refunds create a credit note linked to the original invoice, for the full or the refunded amount. An issued invoice never changes: a correction is always a credit note or a corrected document that points to the original.

6Page builders: widgets and members-only sections

Every Pro adds its widgets to the "Rinnovo" category in Elementor and in the block editor (for example Class schedule, Contract quota, Media library, Bundle builder, Plan table). In Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks, WPBakery, Oxygen or Breakdance, paste the shortcode into a shortcode or text module.

With Membership Content Pro you can also hide or show any part of a page by membership:

  1. Elementor: select the section, container, column or widget (a tab or heading too) › Advanced › Members only (Rinnovo). Choose Show to (logged in, any member, a group, a tier or higher, non-members, expired members, course owners, a download plan) and an optional "Show instead" teaser.
  2. Block editor: select any block › the Members only panel in the sidebar › the same choices.
  3. Click Update. Hidden parts are removed on the server, so they never reach the visitor's browser, and members-only pages are not served from the page cache.

Screenshots of both screens are on the guide page in your account.

7Insights for your dashboard

Each Pro shares read-only numbers with the Performance Insight for WooCommerce dashboard: MRR and ARR, churn, renewals, trials and the Pro's own figures (boxes, members, plays, contract quotas, class utilisation, bundles), plus a list of things to follow up. Only shop managers can see them. Developers can read the same data with rinnovo_insights( $section ) or GET /wp-json/rinnovo/v1/insights?section=sections.
