Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Your login page, your brand, your rules

Sarala Login Page gives every WordPress and WooCommerce site free social login with Google, Facebook, Apple, Microsoft, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Discord, Yahoo and Reddit, plus login security. Pro adds what agencies, shops and growing teams need next.

New: 7 more login providers

  • Slack, TikTok, Twitch, VK (VKontakte), Patreon, Amazon and Disqus, set up next to the free providers with step-by-step instructions for each.
  • Forgery-proof: Slack and Twitch login tokens are checked against their public keys, like Google, Apple and the others.

New: buttons in your brand

  • Four styles: standard full-width buttons, icon tiles, outline boxes or brand pills.
  • Corners: rounded, pill, square or circle.
  • Your colours: background, text, border and hover for every button, or each provider's own colours, with a live preview.

New: a CAPTCHA that never locks you out

  • Cloudflare Turnstile, Google reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha on login and registration, for WordPress and WooCommerce.
  • Automatic backup: if the CAPTCHA can't load or its service can't be reached, the security question appears instead, so a broken CAPTCHA never locks out your customers, or you.

New: stronger security

  • Encrypted keys: provider secrets, the Apple key and the CAPTCHA secret are stored encrypted with your site's own security keys, so a database backup alone doesn't reveal them.
  • Blocked redirects: addresses nobody is ever sent to after logging in or out, whatever a link or another plugin asks for.

Your login page, your brand

  • 5 templates: Glass, Split screen, Wave card, Image card and Gradient, all mobile ready.
  • Your logo, background and side images, colours, fonts, corners and your own texts.
  • Register, forgot password and reset password screens match automatically.

Login redirects and login widget

  • A page for every role after login and after logout: admins to the dashboard, customers to My Account, members to their area.
  • AJAX login widget for sidebars and pages, with social buttons and your own HTML and CSS.
  • Profile pictures: users upload their own picture instead of Gravatar.

A super admin who stays in charge

  • One owner adds and manages every other administrator. Other admins can't edit, delete or demote you, or each other.
  • Optionally only you install plugins and themes, edit files and update WordPress.

Activity log and a cleaner dashboard

  • Every user's logins, edits, plugin changes and WooCommerce orders, with IP and device, filters and CSV export.
  • Hide admin menus, tools, widgets, custom fields, editor blocks and panels per role or per user.
  • Show or hide front-end blocks and widgets for guests, members or chosen roles.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.2 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Sarala Login Page plugin 1.2.0 or later (from WordPress.org). WooCommerce is optional: Pro works with it but doesn't need it.
