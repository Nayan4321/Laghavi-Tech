Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Mega menus your visitors understand at a glance

The free Manohara Menu Design gives desktop, tablet and mobile each a modern menu. Manohara Menu Design Pro adds mega menus, a drag-and-drop builder, icons and badges, shop menus and a smarter header. Everything in the free plugin stays free, and your designs stay as they are.

Mega menus, ready in a minute

  • 19 ready designs: icon cards, platform grid with intro, side tabs with badges, vertical tabs, classic and underlined columns, dark icon tiles, store columns with a banner, category tabs with products, side tabs with a carousel, fashion lists with image tiles, dark grid with a gradient promo and more.
  • Built from your menu in Appearance › Menus: second-level items become columns, cards or tabs, third-level items their links.
  • Intro text with two buttons, a promo card with image, price and button, and a footer strip.
  • Content width, full screen width or a fixed width; the panel always stays on the screen.
  • On phones and tablets, mega menus open as accordions with everything in one column.

Drag-and-drop grid builder

  • Rows of 12 column units; drag rows and blocks to move them.
  • Headings, links with icons and descriptions, a menu, text, images, buttons, promo banners, YouTube, Vimeo or video files, social links, latest posts and shortcodes.
  • Or use a block editor synced pattern or an Elementor template as the whole panel.

Icons, images and badges on menu items

  • 57 built-in icons, WordPress Dashicons, Font Awesome or your own images from the media library.
  • Badges such as New, Popular or -20%, in any colour.
  • The item's description under its label.
  • Hide any item on desktop, tablet or mobile.

WooCommerce menus

  • A cart button with the number of items, updated as soon as a product is added, and a My account button.
  • The item count on menu items that link to the cart.
  • Product grids (popular, latest, best rated, on sale or featured) and product category grids inside mega menus.

A smarter header and menu button

  • The header stays at the top, gets smaller when scrolling, or hides when scrolling down and comes back when scrolling up.
  • Eight menu button styles with their own animation, and text next to the button.
  • A different logo on phones, and the side panel width you want.

Made for phones

  • Swipe the menu panel away to close it.
  • On touch screens, the first tap on a parent item opens its submenu and the second follows the link.
  • Hide theme elements on one device, such as a second theme menu or a desktop-only bar.

Multilingual and right to left

  • Works with Vacanam Language Switcher, Polylang, WPML and TranslatePress.
  • Each language's menu keeps its own icons, badges and mega menus; Vacanam copies them when it copies a menu.
  • Every design works in Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu.

What you need

  • The free Manohara Menu Design (WordPress 6.2 or later, PHP 7.4 or later).
  • Any theme: classic themes, block themes, Elementor and other page builders.
