Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Memberships that fill your classes

Rinnovo Session Membership Pro adds class booking to the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Members pay weekly, monthly or yearly and book without paying each time.

Booking with credits

  • "8 classes a month" or unlimited. Credits refill on renewal and expire, roll over, or roll over up to a limit. Top-ups for extra sessions.
  • A built-in class schedule with Book buttons, or WooCommerce Bookings, Amelia and Bookly.
  • Recurring slots: members reserve "every Tuesday at 17:00" and are booked automatically.

Capacity, waitlists and changes

  • Places per class (1 for private sessions) and a waitlist that books the next person when someone cancels.
  • Cancel or move a session up to the cut-off. Late cancellations and no-shows can use up the credit.
  • Freeze for holidays or injury: the renewal date moves and nothing is charged.

Online sessions and reminders

  • A Zoom meeting for each online session, or a Google Meet, Teams or other link, shown only to booked members.
  • Calendar files and personal calendar feeds for Google, Outlook and Apple.
  • Email and SMS (Twilio) reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.

Studio tools

  • Attendance and progress notes for instructors, and attended classes for members.
  • Family accounts: one parent pays and books for their children with the shared sessions.
  • Waivers and intake forms signed at checkout and stored with date and IP.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products, with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
