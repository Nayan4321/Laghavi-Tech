Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Class bookings that keep every credit right

Rinnovo Session Membership Pro is the classes, sessions and studio memberships branch of the Rinnovo family. Class memberships, packs and passes for studios, gyms, schools and coaches, with a credit ledger, family profiles and QR check-in.

Every pricing model

  • Monthly credits, unlimited, packs with their own expiry, family pools, private sessions, trials, top-ups and credits per class type.
  • Credit ledger: reserve, confirm, deduct or restore, each with a reason; two bookings can never take the same last credit.

Schedule and booking

  • [session_schedule] class cards: time, room or online, instructor, places left, credit cost, Book or Join waitlist, with category and location filters.
  • Recurring reservations (every Monday and Wednesday 6 PM for 4 weeks) that stop when the membership ends.
  • Guest and trial bookings, class-specific restrictions and self-service cancel and reschedule.

Families

  • Each family member has their own profile, bookings, attendance and waiver; shared pool, separate allowances or both.

Classes and instructors

  • Time zone per class, holidays and blackout dates, one-date cancellations and substitute instructors — booked members are told and credits come back.
  • Instructors see only their own classes; progress notes with retention rules.

Check-in and reports

  • QR code or short code check-in; missed-class notifications.
  • Utilisation, attendance, no-shows and late cancels per class; instructor roster with days away.

Connect your booking tool

  • Adapters for WooCommerce Bookings, Amelia and Bookly: bookings, cancellations and reschedules take and return credits, with duplicate protection.
  • Zoom meeting per session, calendar feed and SMS reminders.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
