Subscription boxes that pack and ship on schedule

Rinnovo Physical Subscription Boxes & Goods Pro adds everything a box business needs to the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin: any delivery frequency, self-service skips and swaps, one shipping day, renewal shipping rates, prepaid and gift plans, and packing lists.

Any frequency

  • Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or custom (every 2, 4 or 6 weeks, every 3 months…).
  • Offer several frequencies on one product; customers pick one and can switch later.
  • First box offers: a discount on the first box, a free trial, and a packaging or setup fee with your own name.

Customers manage their own boxes

  • Skip the next box (holiday, too much left over). Nothing is charged; you choose how many skips in a row. Undo any time before the date.
  • Swap products in the next box (another roast, another size) and change quantities.
  • Switch frequency, for example from every 4 weeks to every 6 weeks.
  • One address and one card for all their boxes.
  • Order cut-off: changes close before packing, so a box being packed never changes.

One shipping day

  • Bill every subscriber on the same day of the month (weekly boxes on the same weekday), so boxes are packed and shipped in one batch.
  • Sign-ups after the cut-off get their first box in the next cycle, and checkout tells them when it ships.

Shipping on every renewal

  • Renewal shipping uses your WooCommerce shipping zones: flat rates, table rates and live carrier rates (USPS, UPS, DHL, FedEx). Or keep the first order's shipping, or ship renewals free.
  • Card subscriptions include shipping in the recurring amount, updated in Stripe when the box or address changes.

Prepaid and gift plans

  • Pay once for 3, 6 or 12 boxes, with an optional discount. Each box becomes its own order on its delivery date.
  • A gift message printed on the packing list, and an email inviting the customer to order again with the last box.

Subscribe & Save

  • On any normal product: "One-time purchase" or "Subscribe & save 10%", with a frequency picker. The shop list shows "or subscribe & save 10%".
  • Build-a-box: works with WooCommerce Product Bundles, YITH, WPC, Mix and Match and Composite Products. The chosen items are re-sent every renewal and can be swapped.

Emails and packing

  • Your next box (contents, date, amount, skip or swap button), Your box has shipped (with tracking), Your card expires soon, and Your prepaid plan has ended.
  • WooCommerce > Boxes: orders to pack by ship date with the total of each item (40 × Dark roast), a CSV for your label printer, and renewals for the next 45 days so you can buy stock.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products, with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
