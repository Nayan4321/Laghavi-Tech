Subscription boxes that build, pack and ship on schedule

Rinnovo Physical Subscription Boxes & Goods Pro is the subscription boxes and recurring goods branch of the Rinnovo family. Run a subscription box or a subscribe & save shop: customers build, skip and swap their boxes; you bill on one day, pack from one list and ship.

Guided setup

  • Pick one of three paths: recurring subscription box, subscribe & save on an existing product, or a build-your-own box. Only the settings you need are shown.

Box builder (included)

  • Fixed boxes, pick-your-items boxes, sized boxes (6, 12 or 24 items) and rotating menus with selection deadlines (meal kits, lunch boxes, flower or fruit boxes).
  • Step-by-step builder with a live price, min/max per item, box-specific prices or extra charges and stock checks.
  • Every box is saved as a snapshot of items, variations, quantities and prices.

Frequencies

  • Every week, 2, 4 or 6 weeks, month, 3 months… shown as tap-to-choose chips; customers switch later.
  • First-box discount, free trial and a packaging or setup fee with your own name.

Customers manage their boxes

  • Skip, swap and change the next box before a cut-off, so a box being packed never changes.
  • Swap price rules: charge, refund or credit the difference, or keep the price.
  • Out-of-stock rules: substitute, skip the item or hold the box.

Gifts and prepaid plans

  • Send a box as a gift: recipient name and email, start date, message; the recipient gets their own page.
  • Pay once for 3, 6 or 12 boxes: each box becomes its own order on its date, with the boxes left always visible.

Shipping and packing

  • One shipping day for everyone; renewal shipping from your zones (flat, table or live carrier rates), recalculated when the address changes.
  • Delivery calendar and prepaid tracking screens, orders to pack with item totals, CSV for your label printer, and expected stock for the next weeks.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
