Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Retainers that track every hour and deliverable

Rinnovo Service Contracts Pro is the retainers and service agreements branch of the Rinnovo family. Sell retainers with several allowances, take client requests, log work and send a monthly report — with tax invoices for India, the UAE, the EU and the UK.

Allowances

  • Several allowances per contract, each with a unit, rollover rule, maximum and expiry.
  • A ledger of every use (date, quantity, request, contract): reserved, approved and completed work can't double-spend the balance.

Requests and deliverables

  • Clients submit requests; you approve or reject (unapproved work never uses the allowance).
  • Pending, in progress, review, completed, with revisions counted; work log with staff assignment, notes and attachments.

Monthly service report

  • At the end of each period: 8/10 hours used, 6/8 articles delivered, requests completed and pending — emailed and in the client's account.

Terms and cancellation

  • Minimum term and notice period; cancellation requested, scheduled and terminated.
  • Onboarding fee charged at once, on the first invoice or separately, never prorated; first-month proration by your billing day and time zone.
  • A PDF of the agreed terms for each contract.

Invoices and approval

  • Tax invoices with SAC/HSN, GSTIN and CGST/SGST/IGST, UAE VAT and EU/UK VAT; financial-year numbering.
  • Two approval workflows: the client approves before the invoice is issued, or approves an issued invoice for payment, with history.

Your tools

  • Monthly tasks in Trello or ClickUp, CRM sync and webhooks — credentials encrypted, webhooks signed and retried, tasks never duplicated.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
