Paid memberships for any content

Rinnovo Membership Content Pro adds membership tiers and content protection to the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Sell Silver, Gold and All-Access with any WooCommerce payment method, and protect pages, posts, products, courses, forums, files and videos.

Tiers and rules

  • Tiers that build on each other (Silver, Gold, All-Access), each given by WooCommerce products: subscriptions, Monthly / Yearly variations or one-time purchases for lifetime access.
  • Protect anything: pages, posts, products, any custom post type, categories and tags, or a whole content type. Child pages follow their parent.
  • Parts of a page: the Members only block, a CSS class on any block, or a shortcode. Show something only to non-members too.

Drip, teasers and paywalls

  • Drip: unlock content N days after joining (module 2 after 7 days) or on a date.
  • Teasers: an excerpt, the first words or a blurred preview, then a "Subscribe to unlock" box with the price. The rest never reaches the browser, feeds or the REST API.

Forums, communities and courses

  • bbPress and BuddyBoss forums, BuddyPress / BuddyBoss activity, profiles, groups and messages.
  • LearnDash and LifterLMS: members are enrolled in a tier's courses automatically, and unenrolled when it ends.

Protected files and private video

  • Members-only files: served only to members, so shared links don't work. Audio and video can still seek. A digital library page for each member.
  • Private video: Bunny Stream (signed links), Vimeo, Wistia, YouTube and Presto Player. The player is only in the page for members.

Members love it

  • My Content: membership, renewal date, unlocked content and what unlocks next, courses, files, perks and billing in one place.
  • Upgrades and downgrades between tiers or Monthly / Yearly, with the unused time credited.
  • Member perks: a discount per tier with "Member price" in the shop, free shipping and bonus products.
  • Member directory and badges.

Teams and safety

  • Team seats: a school, company or family buys seats and invites people by email.
  • Access ends at once when a membership is cancelled or refunded, with a grace period for failed payments.
  • No account sharing: a limit on devices logged in and videos playing at the same time.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products, with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
