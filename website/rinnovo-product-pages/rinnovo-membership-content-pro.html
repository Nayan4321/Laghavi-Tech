Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Memberships, courses and downloads that keep access right

Rinnovo Membership Content Pro is the memberships, courses and resource libraries branch of the Rinnovo family. Three businesses on one engine: paid memberships, courses sold as products and download libraries with credits.

Membership groups and tiers

  • Independent programmes (Business training: Silver, Gold, Platinum; Photography: Basic, Premium; VIP community), each with its own tiers.
  • Several memberships at once: access comes from all of them together.
  • Trials, invite-only memberships, gift memberships and membership bundles.

Lifecycle rules

  • What happens on payment, failed payment, grace period, cancel, refund, upgrade, downgrade and expiry — set once, applied everywhere.
  • Upgrades work at once; downgrades at the end of the paid period.

Drip timeline

  • Day 1 welcome, day 7 module 2, day 30 premium resources: content opens by days of membership or on a date, shown as a timeline.

Course commerce

  • Map any product to LearnDash, LifterLMS or a category of course pages: access length, start, lifetime or limited.
  • Course bundles (one product, several courses) and one course unlocked by several products; a course-vs-tier matrix.
  • Products connected to categories: buying the product opens every page in the category.

Download plans and credits

  • A stock-library style resource library: previews, file type, size and credit cost per item.
  • Per-category quotas or one shared credit wallet, reset periods, carry-over, daily limits, and top-up packs that never expire with the monthly reset.
  • Credits are taken in one safe step with a full history; files never have a public URL.

Protected files and video

  • Expiring, checked downloads (X-Sendfile / X-Accel), no-store headers and download limits.
  • Bunny, Vimeo and Wistia privacy per video, resume playback, progress and stream limits (with an honest warning about YouTube unlisted).

My Membership and page builders

  • A My Membership dashboard: plan, renewal, credits left, courses, downloads, quick links.
  • Hide or show any section, column, widget, tab or block by membership in Elementor, the block editor and Bricks, removed on the server; dynamic tags for name, tier, renewal date and credits.
  • REST API, webhooks, member notes, announcements and access logs.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
