Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Rinnovo Pro: get paid, send the invoice, renew. Automatically.

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses turns WooCommerce into a store for subscriptions and license keys, with any payment method in your WooCommerce settings. Pro adds what a growing business needs next: proper tax invoices, offers that bring in new subscribers, VAT for business customers and prices in your customers' currency.

PDF invoices and tax invoices

  • An invoice for every payment. Every paid order and every subscription renewal gets an invoice the moment the payment comes in.
  • Real PDF files named after the invoice number (INV-00001.pdf), attached to the order email and always available in My Account > Invoices.
  • Tax invoices with your TRN. Your legal company name, legal address and tax registration number (TRN, VAT No., GSTIN). With a number, the title reads "Tax Invoice" and tax is shown on every line.
  • Your customer's company. Business customers add their legal company name, address and TRN at checkout or in their account. Optional, required or off: you choose.
  • Three designs (Classic, Bold, Modern) in your colour with your logo. Preview each one as a PDF before you save.
  • Numbers without gaps, with your own prefix and starting number. Each invoice keeps the details it was issued with.

New: free trials and first-payment offers

  • Free trials of any length, with or without a card. With a card, it's saved at sign-up and charged when the trial ends. Without a card, checkout asks for no payment and the customer gets a "Pay now" invoice when the trial ends.
  • A discount on the first payment, as a percentage or a fixed amount ("first month half price"). Every later payment is the full price.
  • Clear prices: "First payment: $5.00" and "No card needed for the trial" under the price, in the cart and at checkout.

New: VAT / GST for digital goods

  • A VAT / GST number field for business customers on the classic and block checkout.
  • EU VAT numbers checked with VIES; UK, Swiss, Norwegian, Indian (GSTIN), Australian (ABN), UAE and Saudi (TRN), Canadian, New Zealand and Singapore numbers checked for the right format.
  • Reverse charge: EU businesses in another EU country pay no VAT. Totals update as soon as the number is typed, and the invoice carries your reverse charge note.
  • Evidence of location: billing country and IP country kept on each order, with mismatches flagged. Every renewal keeps the first order's tax number.
  • Works with your tax plugin: WooCommerce Tax, TaxJar, Quaderno and others.

Currency switcher

  • Show prices in AED, USD, EUR, GBP and more, with automatic daily or manual exchange rates, rounding per currency and a currency per country.
  • Orders, Stripe payments and every renewal stay in the currency the customer chose.

"Ask for price" products

  • Hide the price of any product and show "Ask for price" (your contact or booking page) and "Chat on WhatsApp" with a ready-written message naming the product.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products: Monthly and Yearly plans, trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • License keys, downloads and automatic updates for the plugins you sell, including plans by number of sites (1 / 5 / unlimited).
  • A better My Account and a subscriptions dashboard with monthly recurring revenue.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
