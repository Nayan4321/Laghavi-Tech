Subscriptions and licenses that bill correctly everywhere

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses Pro is the billing, licenses and tax branch of the Rinnovo family. Sell any subscription or software license with WooCommerce: trials that convert, invoices and credit notes, VAT/GST, multi-currency and license keys.

Trials that convert

  • Free or paid trials with or without a card, and first-payment discounts.
  • One trial per customer (by account, email and card where the gateway gives it), so trials can't be repeated.
  • Trial-ending reminder emails, and a clear end date and first renewal amount on the product page, checkout, emails and My Account.
  • You choose what happens when the first payment after a trial fails.

Invoices and credit notes

  • Tax invoices on every order and renewal: 3 templates, your logo and colours, company and tax details.
  • Credit notes for refunds, full or partial, linked to the original invoice.
  • Numbers that never repeat, even with payments at the same moment, with financial-year numbering ({fy}).
  • Issued invoices never change: corrections are credit notes, and each invoice keeps a snapshot of the details at issue time.
  • CSV export of invoices and tax for your accountant.

VAT and GST

  • VAT/GST number at checkout, checked with VIES for EU numbers.
  • Reverse charge by rules: per country, customer type and where you are. A valid number alone doesn't switch tax off.
  • Location evidence (billing address, IP and more) and a record of every tax decision, plus each customer's tax-exemption history.

Currencies

  • Converted prices (rate × base price, daily rates, rounding) or fixed prices per currency.
  • A subscription keeps its currency and amount: rate updates never change an existing renewal.
  • Warnings when a payment method can't charge a currency for renewals; currency switcher widget and shortcode.

Licenses and plans

  • License keys with sites per variation (1 site, 5 sites, unlimited).
  • Licenses follow plan changes: upgrades, downgrades and renewals update sites and expiry, with a full history.
  • Subscriptions per variation (monthly, yearly, lifetime) on one product.

"Ask for price" and quotes

  • Replace the price with an "Ask for price" button that opens your contact page or WhatsApp.
  • Every enquiry is saved: product, variation, quantity, customer and status (new, contacted, quoted, won, lost), with a one-click order.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
