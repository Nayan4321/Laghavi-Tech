Your own subscription streaming and media platform

Rinnovo Media Archives Pro is the media hosting, streaming and distribution branch of the Rinnovo family. Subscription-based media hosting, streaming and digital distribution for podcast networks, video educators, stock media sites, agencies and production houses.

Four business models

  • Video streaming library: browse, search, filter, watch, resume, playlists, favourites and history.
  • Premium podcast network: free and premium episodes, seasons, a private RSS feed per subscriber that you can rotate when it's shared.
  • Stock media library: watermarked previews, collections, download quotas per plan and per asset.
  • Private media portals: client-specific collections and permissions with your own branding.

Security pipeline

  • Identity, plan, device and stream limits are checked, then a short-lived signed link or provider token is issued (Bunny, Cloudflare Stream, S3/CloudFront).
  • Keys stay on the server; signed webhooks with replay protection, rate limits, device identification and configurable geo alerts.
  • Honest by design: signed links are not DRM.

Media account portal

  • Continue watching, video, podcasts and media library, plan and benefits, devices, active streams, downloads, feeds, billing and security alerts.
  • Grid, list or carousel, columns, thumbnail ratio, player colours and separate templates for video, audio and galleries.

Run the library

  • Collections, playlists and seasons, scheduled publishing, free previews, expiring share links and several libraries (brands or clients).
  • Analytics of plays, downloads and watch time; usage add-ons that sell extra streams or downloads.
  • Tabs for Plans & Quotas, Media Providers, Protection, Apps & Integrations, and Security & Activity.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships), Bundles Pro (bundles and plan switching).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
