Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Sell access, not downloads

Rinnovo Media Archives Pro adds protected streaming and app access to the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Subscribers watch, listen and use your app while they pay, and everything stops the moment they don't.

Expiring, tokenized links

  • Bunny Stream and Bunny CDN, Cloudflare Stream, Amazon CloudFront and S3, and Vimeo private links.
  • Links are made for subscribers only and expire. The page source never shows a reusable address.

Protected players and galleries

  • Video and audio players with no download buttons, no right-click and no playing from other sites.
  • A moving watermark with the subscriber's email and IP over the video.
  • View-only galleries: art at screen size only, with the subscriber's email drawn into the image. Originals stay protected.

Private podcast feeds

  • Each subscriber gets their own RSS address for Apple Podcasts, Overcast and other apps.
  • The feed stops the moment the subscription ends, and feeds used from too many places are flagged as shared.

For your app or SaaS

  • Plans with features and quotas: ai_credits=500, exports=yes, projects=10. Quotas reset every billing period.
  • Subscription API: access, plan, usage, sessions and a portal link, plus signed webhooks (active, payment failed, cancelled, expired, paused, access revoked).
  • Single sign-on: WordPress as the log-in for your app with OAuth 2 (PKCE) and JWT, with the plan inside the token.

Account sharing and instant cut-off

  • Limits per plan on devices signed in and streams at once. Use from two countries at the same time is flagged, or signs the account out everywhere.
  • Cancelled, refunded or unpaid: app tokens, feeds, players and devices stop at once.

Trials, upgrades and a self-service portal

  • 7 or 14 day free trials with or without a card, and upgrades with the unused time credited.
  • Your access page: plan, usage, feed addresses, devices and "sign out everywhere", pause, plan changes, card and invoices. Also inside your app.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products, with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
