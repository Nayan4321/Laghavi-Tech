Rinnovo product pages

Seven Pro plugins, each an equal branch of the Rinnovo family and each sold on its own. One WooCommerce product per plugin: open a page, copy each part into the product, and add the images from the matching folder of rinnovo-product-images-2026.zip. Each product is a variable product with the attribute "Sites": 1 site, 5 sites, Unlimited sites, billed yearly.
