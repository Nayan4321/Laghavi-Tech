Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Bundles and plans that customers build and change

Rinnovo Bundles & Variable Subscriptions Pro adds subscription bundles and plan switching to the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin.

Bundles that keep stock right

  • Make a subscription out of other products: "2 × Coffee + 1 × Mug".
  • Every order and renewal adds the items as packing lines, so their stock goes down and your team knows what to pack.
  • Out-of-stock safeguards: critical items block the purchase, and you (and the customer) are warned before a renewal.

Build your own box

  • Customers pick their items with a minimum and maximum (3 to 5), with a different count for each box size.
  • Flat or dynamic price: one box price, or the sum of the chosen items with a bundle discount, on every renewal.
  • Box builder: [bundle_builder] on any page: box size, items, how often, with a live total.

Switch plans fairly

  • Upgrade or downgrade between variations from My Account, with the cost shown before confirming.
  • Card subscriptions pay the prorated difference at once; invoice subscriptions pay it or get a credit.
  • Same-price swaps (size M to L, vanilla to chocolate) and box item changes until a cut-off.
  • Plan table: each variation's period, sign-up fee, trial and payments in one table, with "Compare plans" for shoppers.

One invoice, one renewal day

  • Subscriptions bought together become one: one renewal, one invoice, one card charge.
  • Renew every monthly bundle on the same day; the first payment covers only the days until then.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions on simple and variable products, with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
