Bundles and plans that renew exactly as sold

Rinnovo Bundles & Variable Subscriptions Pro is the bundles and plan switching branch of the Rinnovo family. Subscription bundles with exact component stock, and variable plans customers switch between with clear proration.

Bundles

  • Fixed components, selectable components with min/max, optional fixed-price extras and premium picks that cost more; exclusions for combinations that don't go together.
  • Flat or dynamic price with a bundle discount; you choose how a flat price is split for tax, refunds and reports.
  • Built on the shared Rinnovo box builder (included).

Stock and renewals

  • Exact quantities per component on first orders and every renewal, never taken twice when a renewal is retried.
  • Out-of-stock protection: the renewal is held with a resolution screen: replace, skip the item, partial ship, delay or refund the difference, then release.

Customers

  • Change the next box only, copy the previous one, save favourites, skip or replace one component for the next shipment.

Variable plans

  • A plan table per variation (price, period, sign-up fee, trial), shown to shoppers as a comparison.
  • Monthly ⇄ yearly and Basic ⇄ Premium switching with proration, a new renewal date and the payment adjustment.

Operations

  • Bundle coupons, shipping weight from components, order breakdown, packing list CSV and a replace tool for discontinued products.
  • One renewal statement for several independent subscriptions, without merging their records.

Included in every Rinnovo Pro

  • Invoices on every order and renewal (India GST, UAE VAT, EU/UK VAT with reverse charge, credit notes for refunds) — set up once under WooCommerce › Invoices for all your Rinnovo plugins.
  • Page builder widgets for Elementor and the block editor, plus shortcodes for Divi, Beaver Builder, Bricks and others.
  • Insights data for the WooCommerce performance insights dashboard: MRR, churn, renewals and this plugin's own numbers.
  • Customer guide: step-by-step guides in your account while your license is active.
  • The free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin underneath: subscriptions on any product, Stripe and saved-card renewals, "Pay now" emails for every other payment method, and a better My Account.

Works great with

  • Sarala Login Page: designed log-in, sign-up and account pages with Google, Apple and other social log-ins.
  • Vacanam Language Switcher: a multilingual store, with emails and invoices in the customer's language.
  • WooCommerce performance insights: one dashboard for the store and every Rinnovo plugin, with what to follow up.

The Rinnovo family

Seven Pro plugins, each a full product on its own and each working with the others: Rinnovo Pro (billing, licenses and tax), Boxes Pro (subscription boxes and recurring goods), Membership Pro (memberships, courses and resource libraries), Media Archives Pro (media hosting, streaming and distribution), Service Contracts Pro (retainers and service agreements), Session Pro (classes, sessions and studio memberships).

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying. The download holds two plugins: this Pro and the Rinnovo Box Builder, shared by all Rinnovo Pros.
  • The customer guide in your account while your license is active.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin. Works on its own or next to the other Rinnovo Pro plugins.
