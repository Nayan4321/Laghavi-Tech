Up and running in minutes

1

Install

Download the free plugin, then in WordPress go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and choose the zip. WooCommerce must be active.

2

Payments

Nothing new to set up: subscriptions use the payment methods in WooCommerce → Settings → Payments. Renewals are charged automatically through your card or direct-debit payment plugin, or Rinnovo's own Stripe.

3

Create a plan

Edit a product, tick “Subscription”, choose monthly or yearly. To sell software, tick “Sell a license”, choose what you sell and upload your file.

Needs WordPress 6.0+, PHP 7.4+ and WooCommerce 8.0+. Works with HPOS and the block checkout.

Trials that convert

Questions

Do I need another subscription plugin?

No. Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses handles subscription products, renewals, emails and the customer’s account pages itself. WooCommerce is the only plugin it needs.

Which payment methods can I use?

All of them. Subscriptions use the payment methods in WooCommerce → Settings → Payments, so adding or changing a provider later just works. Renewals are charged automatically with Rinnovo's Stripe and with these payment plugins when they save cards: WooCommerce Stripe Gateway, WooPayments, PayPal Payments, Square, Braintree, Authorize.Net, Mollie, Checkout.com, GoCardless and Paystack. The card or bank mandate is saved at the first payment, and no other subscription plugin is needed. With any other method (bank transfer, Razorpay, a local gateway…) the customer gets a “Pay now” email on each renewal date and pays with any method you offer at that moment.

Can customers cancel by themselves?

Yes, from My Account (you can turn this off). Nothing is charged again, and they keep access until the end of the period they paid for.

I sell software. How do license keys work?

Tick “Sell a license” on the product, choose what you sell and upload your file (zip, exe, dmg, apk, pdf and more) or add a download link. Each paid order gets a key and customers download from My Account. For a WordPress plugin or theme, download a ready-made license file that adds a license screen and automatic updates. Desktop and mobile apps, browser extensions, SaaS, developer tools and games check their key with a few simple web requests; WooCommerce › License guide shows how, with code examples in JavaScript, Python, C# (Unity, .NET), PHP and cURL.

What about digital assets like fonts, templates or presets?

Files can't check a key, so the key is the customer's proof of license, and only customers with an active key can download the files and new versions from My Account. Plugins for creative apps such as Photoshop, Figma or Blender can check their key like any desktop app.

Where do customers find their license key after a renewal?

In My Account › Licenses, with Copy and Download buttons. A renewal keeps the same key and extends it, so nothing changes on their sites. You can revoke, extend or reissue a key (a new key, emailed to the customer) from WooCommerce › Licenses.

Does cancelling stop the payments?

Yes. When a customer cancels in My Account, or you cancel in WooCommerce › Subscriptions, the Stripe subscription is cancelled too, at the end of the paid period or right away. With other payment plugins nothing more is charged, because Rinnovo creates every renewal charge itself.

Do I get tax invoices?

With any Rinnovo Pro, yes. Every paid order and every renewal gets a PDF invoice straight away, attached to the order email and in My Account > Invoices. Add your legal company name, address and TRN and it becomes a “Tax Invoice”; business customers can add their own company name and TRN at checkout. Pick one of three designs and preview it before you save.

What does Subscriptions & Licenses Pro add?

PDF invoices, tax invoices and credit notes for every payment, free trials with or without a card (one per customer), first-payment discounts, VAT / GST numbers with reverse charge rules, “Ask for price” products with contact page and WhatsApp buttons, and a currency switcher with exchange rates and per-country currencies.

Do the Pro plugins need each other?

No. Each Pro (Subscriptions & Licenses, Subscription Boxes, Membership Content, Media Archives, Service Contracts, Session Membership, Bundles) needs only WooCommerce and the free Rinnovo plugin, and each includes invoices. Buy the ones your business needs; they all work together.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time from My Account. Your subscription won’t renew, and your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Nothing is sent to Laghavi Digital. Payments go only to the payment providers you enable in WooCommerce, and the license API runs on your own site.
