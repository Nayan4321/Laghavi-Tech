Rinnovo Pro: get paid, send the invoice, renew. Automatically.

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses (free on WordPress.org) turns WooCommerce into a subscription and license-key store. Pro adds what growing shops need next: proper tax invoices, plans on variable products and selling in your customers' currency.

New: PDF invoices and tax invoices

Stop writing invoices by hand. Every paid order, and every subscription renewal, gets an invoice the moment the payment comes in.

  • Real PDF files, named after the invoice number (INV-00001.pdf), attached to the customer's order email and ready in My Account > Invoices.
  • Tax invoices with your TRN. Add your legal company name, legal address and tax registration number (TRN, VAT No., GSTIN…). With a number the title reads "Tax Invoice", with the tax shown on every line.
  • Your customer's company on the invoice. Business customers add their legal company name, address and TRN in their account or at checkout (classic and block checkout). Make it optional, required, or turn it off and use billing details.
  • Three designs: Classic, Bold (with a "Paid" stamp) and Modern, in your colour, with your logo.
  • Numbers without gaps. Your own prefix, digits and starting number, safe even when two orders are paid at the same moment.
  • Invoices that don't change. Each invoice keeps the details it was issued with. Customer added their TRN later? Refresh the invoice from the order screen, with the same number and date.
  • No extra plugin and no PDF library: it's built in.

Subscriptions on variable products

  • Each variation is its own plan: Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime, each with its own billing period, free trial, sign-up fee and number of payments.
  • Customers pick a plan from one product page.

License keys for variable products

  • Sell one plugin, theme or app as 1 site, 5 sites and unlimited sites, each at its own price.
  • Works together with subscriptions: every plan can be a yearly or monthly subscription with its own license key.

Currency switcher

  • Show prices in AED, USD, EUR, GBP and more, with automatic or manual exchange rates.
  • Pick the currency by country, and let customers pay in their own currency at checkout.

"Ask for price" products

  • Hide the price of any product and show "Ask for price" (your contact or booking page) and "Chat on WhatsApp" with a ready-written message naming the product.

What you get

  • The Pro plugin, installed next to the free Rinnovo plugin. Your settings and data stay as they are.
  • One year of updates and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0+, WooCommerce, PHP 7.4+ and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin.
