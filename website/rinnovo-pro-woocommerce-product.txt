PRODUCT DESCRIPTION  (main editor)
=====================================================================

[Heading 2] Rinnovo Pro: get paid, send the invoice, renew. Automatically.

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses turns WooCommerce into a store for subscriptions and license keys. Pro adds what a growing business needs next: proper tax invoices, plans on variable products and prices in your customers' currency.

[Heading 3] New: PDF invoices and tax invoices

[Bullet list]
Every paid order and every subscription renewal gets an invoice the moment the payment comes in.
Real PDF files named after the invoice number (INV-00001.pdf), attached to the order email and always available in My Account > Invoices.
Your legal company name, legal address and tax registration number (TRN, VAT No., GSTIN). With a number, the title reads "Tax Invoice" and tax is shown on every line.
Business customers add their legal company name, address and TRN at checkout or in their account, for invoices in their company's name. Optional, required or off: you choose.
Payment details are filled in from the order: payment method, date, transaction ID, billing period, automatic renewal and next payment date.
Three designs (Classic, Bold, Modern) in your colour with your logo. Preview each one as a PDF before you save.
Invoice numbers follow on without gaps, with your own prefix and starting number.
Each invoice keeps the details it was issued with. Customer added their TRN after paying? Refresh the invoice from the order screen: same number, same date.

[Heading 3] Subscriptions on variable products

[Bullet list]
Each variation is its own plan: Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime, with its own price, free trial, sign-up fee and number of payments.
Customers choose their plan on one product page.

[Heading 3] License keys for every plan

[Bullet list]
Sell one plugin, theme or app as 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, each at its own price.
Works with subscriptions: every plan can renew yearly or monthly with its own license key.

[Heading 3] Currency switcher

[Bullet list]
Show prices in AED, USD, EUR, GBP and more, with automatic or manual exchange rates.
Choose the currency by country, and let customers pay in their own currency.

[Heading 3] "Ask for price" products

[Bullet list]
Hide the price of any product and show "Ask for price" (your contact or booking page) and "Chat on WhatsApp" with a ready-written message naming the product.

[Heading 3] What you get

[Bullet list]
The Pro plugin, installed next to the free Rinnovo plugin. Your settings and data stay as they are.
One year of updates and email support.
Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.

[Heading 3] Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin.
