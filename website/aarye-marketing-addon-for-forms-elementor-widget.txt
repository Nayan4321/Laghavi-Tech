    <h2>Questions</h2>
    <div class="ar-faq">
      <details><summary>Do I have to change my forms?</summary><p>No. Aarye listens to each form plugin’s own “form sent” step. Your forms, emails and the form plugin’s own entries stay exactly as they are.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Do I have to connect my ad accounts?</summary><p>No. Aarye reads the click IDs and tags that ad platforms already add to every ad click, on your own website. Pasting the tracking template from “Connect your ads” once per ad account only adds the campaign, ad set and ad names.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it track Facebook Instant Forms?</summary><p>No: those forms are filled in inside Facebook and never reach your website. Aarye tracks every visitor who comes to your site, including from ads that open your landing page.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Do I need UTM tags?</summary><p>Not to see the ad network: Google, Meta, Microsoft, TikTok, LinkedIn and others add a click ID to every ad click, and Aarye recognises it. To also see campaign, ad set and ad names, paste the tracking template from “Connect your ads” into your ad account once.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Will it show leads from before I installed it?</summary><p>No, only new ones: the source of a visit can only be read while the visitor is on your site.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it slow my site down?</summary><p>No. The tracking script is a few kilobytes and only reads the page address and referrer. Pro sends leads to your CRM in the background, so forms answer straight away.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Does it work with caching and multilingual sites?</summary><p>Yes. The source is kept in a first-party cookie, so cached pages work, and the language of each lead is saved from WPML, Polylang, TranslatePress, Weglot or the page itself.</p></details>
      <details><summary>Is personal data sent anywhere?</summary><p>The free plugin sends nothing to us. Pro sends leads only to the services you connect. Laghavi Ads Hub receives only the source of each lead, never names, emails, phone numbers or messages.</p></details>
