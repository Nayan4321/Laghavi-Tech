Product descriptionThe main editor at the top of the product

Rinnovo Pro: get paid, send the invoice, renew. Automatically.

Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses turns WooCommerce into a store for subscriptions and license keys, with any payment method in your WooCommerce settings. Pro adds what a growing business needs next: proper tax invoices, plans on variable products and prices in your customers' currency.

New: PDF invoices and tax invoices

  • An invoice for every payment. Every paid order and every subscription renewal gets an invoice the moment the payment comes in.
  • Real PDF files named after the invoice number (INV-00001.pdf), attached to the order email and always available in My Account > Invoices.
  • Tax invoices with your TRN. Your legal company name, legal address and tax registration number (TRN, VAT No., GSTIN). With a number, the title reads "Tax Invoice" and tax is shown on every line.
  • Your customer's company. Business customers add their legal company name, address and TRN at checkout or in their account. Optional, required or off: you choose.
  • Payment details filled in for you: payment method, date, transaction ID, billing period, automatic renewal and next payment date.
  • Three designs (Classic, Bold, Modern) in your colour with your logo. Preview each one as a PDF before you save.
  • Numbers without gaps, with your own prefix and starting number.
  • Invoices that don't change. Each invoice keeps the details it was issued with. A customer added their TRN after paying? Refresh the invoice from the order screen: same number, same date.

Subscriptions on variable products

  • Each variation is its own plan: Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime, with its own price, free trial, sign-up fee and number of payments.
  • Customers choose their plan on one product page.

License keys for every plan

  • Sell one plugin, theme or app as 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, each at its own price.
  • Works with subscriptions: every plan renews yearly or monthly with its own license key.

Currency switcher

  • Show prices in AED, USD, EUR, GBP and more, with automatic or manual exchange rates and rounding.
  • Choose the currency by country, and let customers pay in their own currency. Renewals stay in that currency.

"Ask for price" products

  • Hide the price of any product and show "Ask for price" (your contact or booking page) and "Chat on WhatsApp" with a ready-written message naming the product.

Everything in the free plugin

  • Subscriptions with free trials, sign-up fees and a set number of payments, on any WooCommerce payment method.
  • Automatic Stripe renewals, and "Pay now" renewal emails for every other payment method.
  • A better My Account: next payment, payment history, update card, cancel and resume.
  • License keys, downloads and automatic updates for the plugins you sell.
  • A subscriptions dashboard with monthly recurring revenue.

Your license

  • Choose 1 site, 5 sites or unlimited sites, billed yearly.
  • Automatic updates from your WordPress dashboard and email support.
  • Your license key and download in My Account right after paying.
  • Cancel any time: your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Requirements

WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.0 or later, PHP 7.4 or later, and the free Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses plugin.

  • Unlimited sites: tick Subscription and set it to every 1 year (it is a one-time price now).
