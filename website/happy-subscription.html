Up and running in minutes

1

Install

Download the free plugin, then in WordPress go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin and choose the zip. WooCommerce must be active.

2

Connect Stripe

Paste your Stripe secret key in WooCommerce → Settings → Payments → Stripe. The webhook is set up for you automatically.

3

Create a plan

Edit a product, tick “Subscription”, choose monthly or yearly. To sell software, tick “Sell a license” and upload your zip.

Needs WordPress 6.0+, PHP 7.4+ and WooCommerce 8.0+. Works with HPOS and the block checkout.

Questions

Do I need another subscription plugin?

No. Happy Subscription handles subscription products, renewals, emails and the customer’s account pages itself. WooCommerce is the only plugin it needs.

Which payment methods can I use?

Stripe charges renewals automatically. Any other WooCommerce payment method works with invoice renewals: on each renewal date the customer gets a “Pay now” email.

Can customers cancel by themselves?

Yes, from My Account (you can turn this off). Nothing is charged again, and they keep access until the end of the period they paid for.

I sell plugins. How do license keys work?

Tick “Sell a license” on the product and upload your zip. Each paid order gets a key, customers download from My Account, and your plugin activates the key and gets updates through the built-in license API.

What does Pro add?

Licenses for variable products (for example 1 site, 5 sites and unlimited at different prices), “Ask for price” products with contact page and WhatsApp buttons, and a currency switcher with exchange rates and per-country currencies.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time from My Account. Your subscription won’t renew, and your license keeps working until the end of the year you paid for.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Nothing is sent to Laghavi Digital. Payment details go to Stripe only when you use it, and the license API runs on your own site.
