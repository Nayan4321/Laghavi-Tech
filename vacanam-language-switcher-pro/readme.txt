Pro add-on for Vacanam Language Switcher: AI translation, translated addresses and domains, multilingual WooCommerce with a currency per language, country targeting, string translation and analytics.

== Description ==

Vacanam Language Switcher Pro adds to the free Vacanam Language Switcher. Everything the free plugin does stays free; Pro adds:

1. **Addresses**: translated or shared slugs, translated product and category bases, a sub-domain or a domain per language.
2. **Multilingual SEO**: site-wide title templates of Yoast SEO, Rank Math and SEOPress translated; SEO fields translated with each page.
3. **AI translation** with Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or DeepL, with your own key: pages, Elementor designs, products, categories, menus and texts; tone, Arabic variant, site description and glossary; translation memory.
4. **Text in images**: banners redrawn with their text in the page's language (OpenAI or Google), reviewed before use.
5. **String translation**: every text of the theme and plugins, texts in settings, PO and XLIFF import and export.
6. **WooCommerce**: variations, attributes, grouped products and linked products copied; one price and one stock; the cart follows the language; customer emails and PDF invoices in the order's language; order language in admin; shop pages per language.
7. **A currency per language** (off by default), with Rinnovo Subscriptions & Licenses Pro's currency switcher or Vacanam's own conversion.
8. **Visitor's country**: first-visit redirect to the country's language (never admins, editors, robots, or visitors who chose a language) and content per country.
9. **Forms and emails**: Contact Form 7 and WPForms forms per language; WordPress account emails in the visitor's language.
10. **Advanced switchers**: first-visit suggestion, full-screen chooser, phone bottom sheet, language and currency switcher.
11. **Login pages** (and Sarala Login Page) in the visitor's language.
12. **Translation status**: complete, partly translated, original changed or missing.
13. **Dynamic content**: AJAX, REST and mini-cart requests keep the page's language.
14. **Language analytics**: views, language changes, redirects, abandonment, orders and revenue per language. Daily counters only.
15. **Developer tools**: REST API (`vacanam/v1`), `window.Vacanam` and `vacanam:*` JavaScript events, hooks and `vacanampro_register_language_provider()`.

= External services =

Only when you set them up, with your own keys, Pro sends content to translate to:

* Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) – [terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms), [privacy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
* OpenAI (api.openai.com) – [terms](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use), [privacy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
* Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) – [terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms), [privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* DeepL (api.deepl.com, api-free.deepl.com) – [terms](https://www.deepl.com/pro-license), [privacy](https://www.deepl.com/privacy)

The license key and site address are checked with laghavi.site for updates. Analytics stay in your own database.

== Installation ==

1. Install and activate the free Vacanam Language Switcher.
2. Upload this plugin in Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin and activate it.
3. Enter your license key in Languages > Pro license.
4. Choose your options in Languages > Pro.
